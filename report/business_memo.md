# NYC Airbnb Market Analysis - Business Memo

## Research Question

Where and how should a boutique real-estate investment firm enter the NYC short-term-rental market if it plans to acquire a small portfolio of properties?

The analysis focuses on four decisions:
1. which borough to target;
2. which room type offers the strongest opportunity;
3. what host profile to emulate;
4. whether time-based patterns should affect market entry.

## Evidence Strategy

The dataset does not contain revenue, profit, property cost, or true occupancy. The analysis therefore uses total listings, review scores, reviews per month, estimated nights booked, superhost status, and host listing count as market-size, quality, and activity proxies.

## Borough Recommendation

**Brooklyn** is the strongest starting point.

Manhattan has the largest market, but its scale also implies heavier competition. Brooklyn maintains substantial scale with **13,254 listings** compared with Manhattan's **16,355**, while showing stronger proxy performance:

- average rating: **4.76** in Brooklyn vs. **4.67** in Manhattan;
- average estimated nights booked: **46.55** in Brooklyn vs. **41.74** in Manhattan;
- Brooklyn also leads the superhost page in superhost listing count.

## Host Profile

Among superhost listings, small hosts (1-2 listings) show the strongest performance proxies:

| Host Profile | Listings | Avg. Reviews / Month | Avg. Estimated Nights | Avg. Rating |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Small host (1-2) | 3,396 | 1.81 | 175.81 | 4.89 |
| Mid-size host (3-10) | 2,162 | 1.24 | 106.62 | 4.84 |
| Large host (11+) | 1,431 | 0.59 | 85.98 | 4.82 |

## Recommendation

Prioritize **Brooklyn entire-home / apartment listings** and benchmark operations against successful small hosts.

## Key Uncertainties

The dataset does not provide actual revenue, true occupancy, acquisition cost, operating expense, or complete regulatory risk. Estimated nights booked and review activity should therefore be treated as proxies rather than profitability measures. A final investment decision would require property-level financial data, neighborhood-level analysis, and current regulatory review.
